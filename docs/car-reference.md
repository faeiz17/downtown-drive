The photo proportions were cross-checked against these dimensions. The phone's wide-angle lens makes the overhangs look shorter than they are, so the published dimensions take precedence.

## Exterior colour
- **Dark blue metallic.** It reads as navy in shade and overcast light, and bright cobalt with a slight teal flop in direct sun.
- Measured average of clean paint crops (sRGB):
  - `#203c5d`: driver-side door, overcast (`IMG20260905143401`)
  - `#1e2e45`: rear quarter, shaded (`IMG20260905143039`)
  - **Model base colour: about `#1d3658`**, metallic about 0.45, with a clearcoat. The flop comes from the clearcoat plus a lighter, bluer specular tint.
- The front bumper and hood look slightly more teal/saturated than the doors (`IMG20260926150730`, `IMG20260926153953`), so they were probably resprayed. This is modelled as a small hue shift on the front bumper.
- The paint is glossy but worn on the rear bumper, which has faded patches.

## Wheels and tyres
- **Aftermarket 15" satin/matte-black cross-spoke mesh wheels (BBS RS-GT style)**: 10 twin spokes that split into a Y-mesh, with a small **Mitsubishi triple-diamond centre cap** and 4 lug nuts (`IMG20260926150713`, `IMG20260926153953`).
- Tyres are **Dunlop SP Touring**. In the most recent photos (26 Sep 2026) they have **blue sidewall lettering** ("DUNLOP", "SP TOURING"). The earlier photos show plain black sidewalls.
- There are no visible brake calipers through the mesh. The calipers are dark, so they're modelled as dark grey.

## Front
- **Grille:** split black grille with **vertical slats** either side of a body-colour V-shaped nose, with a **chrome Mitsubishi triple-diamond** on the nose (`IMG20260919143255`, `IMG20260926150730`).
- **Headlights:** stock facelift units with a swept-back teardrop shape, a clear polycarbonate lens (slightly hazy/yellowed), a chrome reflector and projector-style bowl, and an **amber indicator bulb in the inner corner** (`IMG20260926154019`, `IMG20260926153913`).
- **Bumper:** body colour, with a **wide horizontal lower intake** and small horizontal **slotted vents** in each lower corner (no fog lamps). The front plate sits on an oversized **black plastic holder** mounted a little proud of the bumper.
- **Wear:** scuffs and scratches on the bumper's right-front corner and lower lip (`IMG20260926150730`, `IMG20260919143255`), and chips on the hood's leading edge.

## Side profile
- CS sedan greenhouse with **chrome window surrounds** on all four doors and **smoked door visors (rain guards)** above the windows (`IMG20260926154027`, `IMG20260919143133`).
- **Chrome door handles** (`IMG20260905143401`, `IMG_20260919_182747`).
- A thin **chrome strip along the bottom of the doors**, above a body-colour rocker.
- **Round side repeaters** (clear lens, amber bulb) on the front fenders behind the wheel arch.
- **Body-colour mirror caps** on black bases.
- The **fuel door** is on the **left** rear quarter, round-cornered (`IMG20260905143401`).
- **Black mud flaps** behind the front and rear wheels (most visible at the rear).
- Ride height is stock and wheel fitment is flush.

## Roof
- **Glass tilt/slide sunroof** at the front of the roof (`IMG20260926150530`, `IMG20260926154027`, `Picsart_26-07-26_16-14-28-903`).
- **Short black whip antenna** on the rear centre of the roof, just ahead of the rear screen, angled back (`IMG20260822135448`, `IMG_20260605_235410`).

## Rear
- **Tail lights:** aftermarket **chrome/clear "Altezza-style" units**, each with **two round red lenses** in the upper section and a **clear lower strip** (reverse light and indicator) (`IMG_20260605_235410`, `IMG20260905143039`).
- **Spoiler:** aftermarket **gloss-black wing on the trunk lid**, with slightly raised end plates and a ducktail profile (`IMG20260822135448`, `IMG20260905143039`).
- **Badges:**
  - chrome **Mitsubishi triple-diamond** centred on the trunk lid, above the plate recess
  - **"TEXAS EDITION"** chrome badge on the upper left of the trunk lid (dealer/aftermarket)
  - **"LANCER"** script at the lower left
  - **"GLX"** at the lower right
- A recessed plate area with a chrome/light trim bar on the trunk lid.
- Exhaust: a single tip, mostly hidden under the right side of the bumper.
- **Wear:** scuffs and small paint chips on the lower rear bumper (centre and right), and faded clear coat.

## Glass and tint
- **Rear screen: dark tint** (near-black from outside, `IMG20260822135448`).
- Side windows: light-to-medium tint.
- Windscreen: clear, with **small stickers in the top corners** (token-tax / parking stickers, including a round green one) (`IMG20260926154027`).

## Number plate
- **Current plate (used in game): white Punjab plate**, black characters "**DAK 539**", a "**PUNJAB**" header strip, a small **ET&NC** emblem on the right of the header, and the Punjab emblem on the left. Front and rear are the same.
- An older green plate ("BHC 515") appears in June 2026 edits. It is superseded and not used.

## Interior (for the interior camera)
- **Beige/cream leather seats with vertical pleats**, front and rear.
- **Beige steering wheel**, beige door cards with light-grey inserts, beige headliner.
- Dark grey/black upper dashboard, beige lower dash.
- Warm orange dome light (`IMG20260919180919`).
